About Care Dot

Care Dot is designed to keep parents seamlessly connected with their children safely and securely. Without the hassle of complex username and password sign-ups, our application uses a simple, highly secure pairing mechanism to bridge parent and child devices instantly.

Key Features & Functionality

Secure Pairing Code

No traditional logins required. Connect parent and child devices securely using a private pairing code system.

Real-Time Location Tracking

Monitor your child's precise live location continuously to ensure their safety while they are away.

Emergency SOS Alerts

Children can trigger an instant SOS alert with a single tap, immediately notifying the parent device during emergencies.

Device Status Monitoring

Keep track of essential device health metrics like battery level and connection status right from the parent app.
